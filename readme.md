```mcfunction
/give @s minecraft:gold_nugget[minecraft:custom_model_data={strings:["tmep:one_ring"]}]
```

## Custom Model Data

### Rings

*gold_nugget.json:*
- The One Ring – `tmep:one_ring`
- Lesser Rings – `tmep:lesser_ring`

*iron_nugget.json:*
- Dwarven Ring – `tmep:dwarven_ring`

- Ring of Men – `tmep:man_ring`
- Narya – `tmep:narya_ring`
- Nenya – `tmep:nenya_ring`
- Vilya – `tmep:vilya_ring`

### Staffs

*shield.json:*
- Gandalf's Staff – `tmep:gandalf_staff`
- Saruman's Staff – `tmep:saruman_staff`
- Radagast's Staff – `tmep:radagast_staff`
- Generic Staff 1 – `tmep:generic_1_staff`
- Generic Staff 2 – `tmep:generic_2_staff`
- Generic Staff 3 – `tmep:generic_3_staff`
- Generic Staff 4 – `tmep:generic_4_staff`
